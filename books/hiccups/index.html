The Malamelo Collection

Hiccups Against Godhood

Working on Draft 3

Beles’s first week as captain is going terribly. Already struggling to earn the respect of her crew, she reluctantly agrees to feed a mysterious blue liquid to Malamelo—the sea behemoth carrying her towering city across the ocean.

Chaos erupts when Malamelo starts glowing and hiccuping, causing city-wide earthquakes and the hasty formation of a cult convinced these are signs of divine punishment.

Beles must figure out where the potion came from and find an antidote before Malamelo shakes the city off its back. Soon, she is sought out by a peculiar woman who speaks strangely, knows far too much, and casually threatens to kill her.
