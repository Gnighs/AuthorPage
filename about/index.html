About Pau

My name is Pau Roca Pardo (POW ROCK-uh PAR-doh). That’s one given name and two surnames—no middle name. You can think of it as a double-barreled name with an imaginary hyphen between the two surnames, which is why I publish as Pau Roca-Pardo.

I’m a Catalan author, born in 2003, who writes fiction in English.
