About Pau

My name is Pau Roca Pardo (POW ROCK-uh PAR-doh). That’s one given name and two surnames—no middle name. You can think of it as a double-barreled surname with an imaginary hyphen, which is why I publish as Pau Roca-Pardo. It just makes it easier for the international audience.

I've been told I'm supposed to write this in third person for professionalism, so I'll start that now.

Pau Roca-Pardo was born in Mataró, Catalonia, in 2003. He is a computer engineering researcher based in Barcelona who writes comic speculative fiction with a fondness for the absurd and ridiculous.
